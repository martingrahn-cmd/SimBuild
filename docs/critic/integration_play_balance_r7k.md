# r7k ordinary-play balance local critical review

**Decision: accepted. No whole-game visual score is assigned.**

The previous economy made the earned 404-resident city insolvent even at 20% tax. The dominant 1,548/day charge treated privately zoned buildings as city-owned expenses while also taxing them, and the flat 1,500 administration cost was disproportionate at village scale. r7k corrects those ownership and scaling errors without granting money, changing facility costs, reducing road upkeep or bypassing the simulation.

The result supports meaningful play. Ten percent gives the player 32 days to respond to an intentionally overbuilt service stock; 15% preserves positive cash for at least 90 unattended days; 20% earns money while producing a measurable happiness penalty. Population, demand, services and lot exhaustion continue through public deterministic steps. Exact repeat runs pass.

Accept r7k. Do not mark ordinary progression complete: the fixture exhausts existing zoning at 465 residents, default 10% still fails after day 33 without player action, and no objective probe substitutes for a human assessment of pacing, clarity and construction choices. The change closes guaranteed short-run insolvency, not the final playtest gate.
