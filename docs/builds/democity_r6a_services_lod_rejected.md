# Democity r6a services LOD candidate — rejected

The accepted 800 m service-detail threshold was tested at 720 m. The candidate preserved the real 31 facilities, coverage, public API, double deserialization and tour contract, and its inspected aerial-noon capture was zero-error.

It did not move any relevant 128 m service chunk into a different LOD bucket at the measured aerial camera. Whole-scene aerial-night geometry measured 3,097,682 triangles versus the accepted r5x 3,097,502 baseline, a negligible 180-triangle run variation rather than a saving. The services contribution remained about 721.5k triangles.

The candidate is rejected and the verified 800 m threshold restored. A threshold change without a chunk transition has no benefit, while a much shorter threshold already failed visually at 480 m.
