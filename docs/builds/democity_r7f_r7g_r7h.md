# Democity r7f/r7g/r7h cross-seed lifecycle repair and service-buffer compression

r7f turns the independent cross-seed rejection into a reproducible public probe. A fresh seed-7 city had 646 roads, 10,113 zone cells and 648 buildings, while restaging seed 7 on the original seed-1337 page initially retained the old terrain basis and produced only 628 roads, 10,033 cells and 616 buildings. District metadata matched, but terrain, road topology and authored counts did not. This was a real lifecycle defect, not seed variation.

r7g adds an owner-safe regeneration path. Terrain rebuilds height, flow, features and all dependent material masks from the same root/terrain RNG stream used by a fresh page. Roads reset their terrain-query cache and permit ID reset only for this new-world authoring path; ordinary deserialization retains its monotonic-ID contract. Democity invokes those owner APIs only when the requested showcase seed differs from the page world seed. The final probe compares all 263,169 terrain samples and finds zero differences, with exact terrain, feature, road-node, road-design, district and count hashes between fresh and restaged seed 7. The exact 1337→7→1337→7 cycle, public API, double deserialize and eight-stop tour also pass.

Correctly rebuilding the larger seed-7 world exposed a lifecycle-memory regression that the old, incorrect restage had hidden. Seed 7 owns 648 buildings, 646 roads and 31 services; after r7g alone its forced-GC heap was 528.4–529.4 MB, above the 512 MB gate. r7g therefore was not accepted by itself.

r7h addresses the measured retained owner without changing facility geometry or service behavior. Service positions remain 32-bit. Static normals move to normalized signed 16-bit storage, UV and service UV to normalized unsigned 16-bit, vertex colour to normalized unsigned 8-bit, service lighting to normalized signed 16-bit, and exact category/surface selectors to unsigned 8-bit. Coverage, IDs, footprints, visibility, shadows, raycasting and serialized state are unchanged.

Seed-7 service CPU buffers fall from 43,949,092 to 22,662,136 bytes. The final correct seed-7 forced-GC result is 509.1 MB with 457,227,077 bytes of backing storage, so the lifecycle memory gate passes even for the larger world. Exact baseline/candidate civic captures differ by only 0.00570824% at noon and 0.0051422% at night. Directed civic, aerial and bridge captures and the complete 16-image matrix were inspected without UV, normal, colour, lighting or terrain corruption.

The final matrix has zero errors, 532 maximum draws, 2,997,270 maximum triangles and 48.1 fps minimum. Production build passes 163 modules. The combined r7g+r7h source is accepted because it closes a functional cross-seed defect and preserves the measured geometry and memory gates. Democity remains **6.0 FAIL**: 50 fps, city scale, lighting, seating, repetition/grain, activity, ordinary progression and whole-world atomic restore remain unresolved. Seed spatial variation also still fails: zero of nine district centroids move more than 50 m between seeds 1337 and 7.
